## What the proposal does establish at design level

The per-invocation trace is proportionate when confined to existing consumed outcomes and exact post-await rawManage/status sites. A scoped AsyncLocalStorage.run can isolate concurrent captures; absent-store application calls can remain inert. The design requires the right controls: original values/errors/result identity, once-call/await counts, nested/concurrent isolation, no public DTO change, limit16/truncation and closed own-data enums/SQLSTATE only. These controls are planned, not executed. No generic client/RPC/thenable wrapper is justified. Accessor/proxy/hostile objects must remain UNKNOWN without executing traps or serializing them.

The SQL oracle is correctly narrower than a JWT/PostgREST replay. A dedicated setup LOGIN+SUPER client can set local session authorization, and verifying session_user/current_user=authenticated, nonSUPER/nonBYPASS plus owned auth.uid/claim predicates immediately before invocation makes the outside caller restricted. SECURITY DEFINER then intentionally changes current identity to the real feed owner; setup SupAdmin7 is not the actual ordinary RPC identity. This cannot reconstruct historical session claims, clock, proof or snapshot. A fresh 60-second proof and Repeatable Read do not establish equivalence by themselves.

The unmodified denial gate, same-input/snapshot paired probe, preserved OID/owner/ACL/proconfig/signature/attributes and exact inverse checks are valuable. Expected-error ROLLBACK TO SAVEPOINT before further SQL, full rollback before identity recovery after unexpected abort, settled queries, same-client and independently qualified fresh-client catalog/owned-row checks, explicit connection close and retained uncertainty are appropriate requirements. A cleanup success cannot disprove an uncertain earlier commit. No PostgreSQL execution was done here to qualify any of them.

The proposal correctly separates transaction-local DDL/rows/claims from committed wrapper key custody, auth/fixture/PostgREST operations, sequence advancement, entropy, clock, files and CI lifecycle. One worker, no file parallelism, no other consumers, no fault/barrier DDL overlapping INT016 and bounded lock timeout are required. Existing Vitest projects are 159 parallel then 6 serial files by groupOrder; normal full-run preservation does not itself prove exclusivity for the new diagnostic case. Implementation must demonstrate INT016's explicit-mode selection and no accidental mutation on ordinary runs, while retaining all normal tests and honestly distinguishing diagnostic additions from the 95 feature inventory.
