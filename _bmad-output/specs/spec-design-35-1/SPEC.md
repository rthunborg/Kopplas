# Story35.1 — Authority And Base Contract

## Why

The existing documentary authority/base contract has seven evidenced outputs but no established formal completion. Adopt it without losing detailed requirements or confusing dated preparation with current admission. Planning/test-design reviewers need a source-pinned, scoped handoff; existing-evidence.md supplies the full preserved criteria, provenance and remaining duties.

## Capabilities

- **CAP-1 — Authority coverage**
  - **intent:** Reviewers can trace every required authority row and journey to a retained, future, excluded or owned destination.
  - **success:** E35-1-AC1/T-AC1 preserve17 MAIN,eight FIELD,five frozen PhaseA journeys,B1–B7 and the atomic requirement-transfer/incoming-link gate.

- **CAP-2 — Dated source provenance**
  - **intent:** Reviewers can distinguish dated checkout, inventory, remote and current preparation facts.
  - **success:** E35-1-AC2/T-AC2 retain exact dates/revisions/hash provenance and missing evidence without converting reservations or historical observations into current admission.

- **CAP-3 — Scoped handoff**
  - **intent:** Each slice has an actionable documentary, concrete-design, material-contract or dependency-bound handoff.
  - **success:** E35-1-AC3/T-AC3 preserve independent E35 progress, acceptedDR-B1–B7 and remaining engineering/dependency limits without approving implementation.

- **CAP-4 — Checkable identities**
  - **intent:** Reviewers can validate current adapter links/output identities while retaining historical input pins.
  - **success:** E35-1-AC4/T-AC4 expose source drift and raw/LF/Git distinctions; identity alone grants no readiness or correctness.

- **CAP-5 — Applicability**
  - **intent:** Each requirement belongs only to its actual owning slice.
  - **success:** E35-1-AC5/T-AC5 preserve27-spec Applies/Inherited/N-A classification and reasons without inventing UI, file, command, viewport or runtime obligations for35.1.

- **CAP-6 — Preservation and ownership**
  - **intent:** Original requirements, unresolved technical blockers, evidence and custody survive the handoff.
  - **success:** E35-1-AC6/T-AC6 retain dated eight-section E17/E18 sketch/EP06 preservation proof and follow-ups with no claim/release/E15/tracking mutation.

- **CAP-7 — Honest evidence**
  - **intent:** Consumers can distinguish documentary checks, planned tests, historical runtime and future release duties.
  - **success:** E35-1-AC7/T-AC7 preserve original money/provenance/projection/transaction/release requirements without inferring runtime/test-design PASS, implementation admission or release approval.

## Constraints

- Preserve the verbatim E35-1-AC1–AC7 meanings and corresponding T-AC1–T-AC7 documentary identities in existing-evidence.md; detailed incorporated requirements remain binding with their owners.
- D1c53833 is the actual preparation base only. Preserve all dated source distinctions, raw/LF/Git identities, disclosed epics drift and original approval limits. Do not rewrite reserved originals or historical input pins.
- Exactly one existing story35-1 is adopted under the scoped run instruction; no new decomposition, checkpoint preference, claim, runtime gate or historical execution phase is created. Execution story/admission/current CI/DONE remain future normal operations.
- Existing acceptedDR-B1–B7 remain settled; engineering/dependency/verification boundaries remain with their actual owners. Product/CRM/PR151/E15/scheduler/claim holds are unchanged.

## Non-goals

- UI/server/schema/migration/Auth/application tests, infrastructure/dependency/config/helper/pin changes, other19/E17/E18 story imports, shared source edits, tracking or claim release.
- Runtime/readiness/release approval, automatic product resumption, final PhaseB consolidation or fabricated historical acceptance.

## Success signal

The ordinary admitted documentary result preserves all seven criteria, binds source and evidence honestly, passes actual applicable documentary checks and independent review, and records current completion through Root’s normal route. This source preparation alone satisfies none of those formal completion transitions.
